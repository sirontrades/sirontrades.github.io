Privacy policy

S.I.R.O.N. is a private assistant used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar solely to answer the owner's requests. Data is processed on the owner's own computer, is not sold or shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising. Mail is never sent automatically; the assistant can only create drafts. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
